Egg & sperm donation

When using your own cells isn't possible, a donor program offers a real chance at pregnancy. We work only with fully screened donors and handle the entire legal side for you.

Egg donation

A donor egg program is recommended for diminished ovarian reserve, premature menopause, genetic risks, or after several unsuccessful IVF attempts with your own eggs.

  • Donors aged 18 to 32 who have healthy children of their own
  • Full medical and genetic screening of every donor
  • Phenotype matching: height, eye and hair color, ethnicity, blood type
  • Fresh and vitrified eggs

Sperm donation

Used for severe male-factor infertility, genetic risks, and in programs for single women.

  • Cryopreserved material with quarantine
  • Donors screened for infections and genetic conditions
  • Detailed anonymized profiles for matching

Donor catalog

Anonymized donor profiles will be available in your patient account after registration and a doctor's consultation. This is a confidentiality requirement: donor and patient data are protected by law.

Anonymity & the law

Donation in Kazakhstan is governed by the Code «On People's Health and the Healthcare System». A donor has no rights or obligations toward the future child. All contracts are drawn up by the clinic's lawyer.

How the program works

Stages of a donor program

Consultation

The fertility specialist confirms the indications and explains your program options.

Donor matching

You choose a donor from anonymized profiles with your coordinating doctor's guidance.

Synchronization & fertilization

Endometrial preparation, fertilization of donor cells, and embryo culture.

Transfer & support

Embryo transfer, early pregnancy support, and pregnancy care at the clinic.

Let's discuss your situation confidentially

Donor program consultations are one-on-one with a doctor. Nothing is shared with third parties.
