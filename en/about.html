The clinic where families begin

TEFIA Clinic is a full-cycle reproductive medicine center in Almaty. We help couples become parents, from finding the cause of infertility to caring for the pregnancy.

Who we are

TEFIA Clinic was founded by doctors and embryologists with years of experience in fertility clinics across Ukraine and Europe. We brought to Almaty what genuinely works: European treatment protocols, strict embryology standards, and real respect for the patient.

We are a small clinic by choice. Each couple has one doctor who knows their story from the first consultation to delivery. There is no assembly line here, but there are results: 9 out of 10 of our patients become moms.

What we stand for

  • Honesty from day one. We tell you your real chances and never promise the impossible.
  • Nothing unnecessary. We only order tests and procedures that actually affect the outcome.
  • European protocols. We follow the guidelines of European reproductive medicine societies (ESHRE).
  • One center for the whole journey. Diagnostics, lab work, embryology, genetics, cryobank, and pregnancy care, all under one roof.

The clinic in numbers

9 out of 10 patients make it all the way to a baby. Senior doctors with 15+ years of experience. We serve patients in Russian, Kazakh, English, and Chinese.

Full range of fertility treatment

IVF, ICSI, donor programs, surrogacy, PGT testing, freezing, and biomaterial storage.

TEFIA CLINIC operates in full compliance with the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan on healthcare and the protection of personal medical data.
