Meet our team

Each specialist owns their stage of the program, and together we guide you through the whole journey, from diagnostics to delivery.

Fertility specialist & OB-GYN, highest category

Dr. Anar Malagazhdarova

The clinic's lead fertility specialist. She designs stimulation protocols, performs egg retrievals and embryo transfers, and leads IVF, ICSI, frozen transfer, and donor programs.

Embryologist

Vladimir Chistyakov

He runs the heart of the clinic: the embryology lab. Fertilization, embryo culture, vitrification, and transfer preparation. Experienced in Ukrainian fertility clinics.

OB-GYN, highest qualification category

Dr. Zhazira Oksikbayeva

She cares for pregnancies after fertility treatment, including the early weeks and post-transfer support, staying with you until delivery with your full history in mind.

Endocrinologist

Dr. Ulzhan Izbasar

She fine-tunes your hormones before and during the program: thyroid, insulin resistance, PCOS. Endocrine health directly affects IVF success.

Urologist-andrologist

Dr. Yelemes Zharylkap

Diagnosis and treatment of male-factor infertility: semen analysis, hormonal issues, and preparation for IVF and ICSI.

Ultrasound specialist

Dr. Nazokat Irisbekova

Follicle monitoring during stimulation, folliculometry, and pregnancy screenings. Expert imaging at every stage of the program.

Internist, highest qualification category

Dr. Ryskul Mussayeva

She assesses your overall health before the program: clearance for IVF and anesthesia, management of chronic conditions, and support throughout pregnancy.
