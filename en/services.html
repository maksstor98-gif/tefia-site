Services & programs

Fertility programs

Assisted reproductive technology

IVF (in vitro fertilization)

The core program: ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, lab fertilization, embryo culture, and transfer. The protocol is tailored to your age, ovarian reserve, and treatment history.

ICSI

A single sperm is injected directly into the egg. Used for male-factor infertility, low sperm quality, or after unsuccessful conventional IVF attempts.

Natural-cycle IVF

A program without hormonal stimulation, or with minimal support. A good fit for patients for whom stimulation is contraindicated or unwanted.

Frozen embryo transfer

A thawed embryo is transferred in a prepared cycle. It gives your body time to recover after stimulation and often improves implantation chances.

Diagnostics

Workup & preparation

Infertility workup

A complete couple's workup: hormone panel, ultrasound, semen analysis, ovarian reserve testing (AMH), and tubal patency.

All lab tests & exams

All samples are collected in-clinic, from routine labs to extended pre-IVF panels. No queues, no trips across town.

Expert-level ultrasound

Folliculometry, stimulation monitoring, and pregnancy screenings on modern equipment.

Specialist consultations

Fertility specialist, endocrinologist, urologist-andrologist, OB-GYN. Every specialist who affects your fertility, in one place.

Male infertility treatment

Semen analysis with morphology, andrologist-led treatment, and sperm selection for ICSI.

Pregnancy care

Pregnancy care programs after IVF or natural conception: a visit schedule, screenings, lab tests, and a doctor who stays in touch.
