FAQ

Before your first visit

How do I book a consultation?
Message us on WhatsApp at +7 776 591 99 91 or give us a call. Our coordinator will find a convenient time and tell you which documents to bring.
What should I bring to the first appointment?
Any medical records, test results, and protocols from previous programs, if you have them. The fuller the history, the more precisely the doctor can assess your situation at the very first meeting.
Should I come alone or with my partner?
Together is better: infertility is a couple's matter, and both partners usually need a workup. But if your partner can't make it, come on your own and we'll build the plan step by step.
Can I get a consultation online?
Yes. For out-of-town and international patients, the first consultation is held over video. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll set a time.
