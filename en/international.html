Patients from abroad

Couples come to us from China, Russia, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan, Turkey, and Europe. We organize the program so you spend minimal time in Almaty and get the maximum result.

Why Kazakhstan

Fertility care without barriers

Legal by law

Donor programs and surrogacy are legal in Kazakhstan, while many countries ban or restrict them. Everything is done officially.

Affordable prices

IVF in Kazakhstan costs 2–3 times less than in Europe, with European-level protocols and lab standards.

Easy logistics

Direct flights to Almaty from major cities in Asia and Europe. Visa-free entry for citizens of many countries. The clinic is 20 minutes from the city center.

4 languages

Consultations and support in Russian, Kazakh, English, and Chinese. Medical records are translated for use in your country.

Start remotely

Your first consultation happens online. You can do the workup at home and come to Almaty only for the treatment stage.

Help with your trip

We'll help you find accommodation near the clinic, meet you at the airport, and build your visit schedule around your travel dates.

How it works

The program for an international patient

Online consultation

You send your records via WhatsApp; the doctor reviews your history and holds a video consultation.

Workup at home

We send you the list of tests; you do them in your own country and send us the results.

Arrival in Almaty

We plan a 10–14 day visit around stimulation, egg retrieval, and transfer.

Remote follow-up

After you return home, we stay in touch and support your early pregnancy online.

For patients coming for donor programs or surrogacy, the clinic's lawyer prepares all documents in advance, tailored to your country's requirements.

Message us in the language you prefer

WhatsApp: Russian, English, Chinese. We reply within one business day and put together your program plan.
