Cryobank & biomaterial storage

Freeze time itself. Cryopreservation keeps eggs, embryos, and sperm safe for years to come: for future programs, delayed parenthood, or before treatment that may affect fertility.

What we store

Cryopreservation programs

Eggs

Delayed motherhood: preserve your eggs while you're young and their quality is at its peak. With vitrification, over 90% of cells survive thawing.

Embryos

Embryos left over from an IVF cycle are stored for future transfers. One stimulation cycle can give you more than one child.

Sperm

Fertility preservation before cancer treatment, surgery, or long trips away. Samples are stored in individual cryo-carriers.

How we keep it safe

  • Vitrification: ultra-rapid freezing with no ice crystal formation
  • Storage in liquid nitrogen at −196 °C
  • Individual labeling and double identification of every sample
  • Continuous monitoring of nitrogen levels and storage conditions
  • Backup Dewar tanks for emergencies

Duration & renewal

Your biomaterial can be stored as long as you need, from a few months to decades. The storage contract renews annually, and we remind you before it expires by phone and WhatsApp.
